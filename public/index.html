            Intelligent Route Navigator & Graph Visualizer
          

        

        
      

      

        

          

            navgraph dsa is an intelligent spatial routing engine built for Data Structures and Algorithms Experiments 14 & 15 (Minor Project).
          

          

            

              
              Dijkstra's Algorithm: Custom Min-Heap Priority Queue for guaranteed optimal shortest cost on weighted graphs.
            

            

              
              A* Heuristic Search: Great-circle Haversine distance heuristic h(n) for directed goal-seeking.
            

            

              
              BFS & DFS Traversal: Breadth-First fewest-hops and Depth-First reachability exploration.
            

            

              
              Interactive Step Player: Step-by-step min-heap frontier, closed visited set, and edge relaxation playback.
